# What's new in Orbit

Releases are published here and in the Microsoft Store. Watch this repository (**Watch › Custom › Releases**) to be notified.

## Coming next

- **Orbit Pro:** an optional one-time upgrade with file conversion, text tools, snippets, macros, clipboard history, more slices, unlimited app wheels and every look. Try it free for a day.
- **Help & feedback** inside Settings: quick answers and a private report form with a "See what's sent" preview.

## 1.1.0

- **Convert files** from the wheel over File Explorer: pictures to PDF, PNG, JPG or WebP (including HEIC and RAW), combine pictures into one PDF, PDF pages to pictures, video to MP4 or a smaller 720p copy, audio to MP3 or WAV, Word / Excel / PowerPoint to PDF, rotate, make an icon, strip hidden info. Works on the file under the pointer or on every selected file.
- **The Files wheel** opens by itself over File Explorer.
- **Slices** wheel shape, alongside One wheel and Tiles.
- **Home page** in Settings with your live wheel, and **settings search** (Ctrl+F).
- **Gallery** with 25 ready-made rings and ready-made wheels for popular apps; add to any wheel; reorder wheels.
- Text stays readable on any accent colour; new logo.

## 1.0.0

- First release: the radial wheel at your pointer, opened by a shortcut or a mouse button, with apps, files, folders, websites, shortcuts, media keys, window snapping, rings inside rings, app wheels, themes and liquid glass.
