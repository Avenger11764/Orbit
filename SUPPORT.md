## Report a problem or suggest an idea

| Where | Visibility | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Orbit › Settings › **Help & feedback** | Private, to the developer | Bugs, with an optional privacy-safe log |
| [orbit-official-website.vercel.app/support](https://orbit-official-website.vercel.app/support) | Private, to the developer | When Orbit itself won't start |
| [GitHub issues](https://github.com/Avenger11764/Orbit/issues/new/choose) | **Public** | Bugs, ideas and questions others can follow and 👍 |

Before opening an issue, please search [existing issues](https://github.com/Avenger11764/Orbit/issues). Include your Orbit version (Settings › About) and Windows version.

## Orbit Pro and the Microsoft Store

Purchases and refunds of Orbit Pro are handled by the Microsoft Store. If Pro doesn't turn on after buying, open Settings › **Orbit Pro** › **Restore purchase**. For refunds, open your Microsoft account's [order history](https://account.microsoft.com/billing/orders) and choose **Request a refund** next to the purchase.

## Privacy requests

To see or delete a report you sent, follow [privacy requests](https://orbit-official-website.vercel.app/privacy-requests).
